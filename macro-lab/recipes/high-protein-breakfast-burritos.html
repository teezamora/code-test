⚡ Bodybuilder Notes
Solid protein punch: ~31g protein per burrito from the egg + sausage + cheese combo — a strong start for muscle protein synthesis first thing in the morning.

Fat is the dominant macro at 54% of calories, mostly from eggs, sausage, and cheddar. To lean it out: swap sausage for lean ground turkey, use reduced-fat cheese, or skip the cream cheese — that alone saves ~33g fat across the whole batch.

Sodium alert: At ~1,388mg per burrito before toppings, this is high. The added salt (2,360mg) and cheddar (1,700mg) are the biggest drivers. Skip the added salt if you're cutting or watching sodium.

Cholesterol is high — 12 eggs contribute the bulk of ~466mg per serving. Fine for bulking phases, but consider using a mix of whole eggs and egg whites if eating daily.

Meal prep MVP: Wrap individually in foil, freeze, bake from frozen at 425°F for 20 min. A week of high-protein breakfasts ready to go — no excuses for skipping the anabolic window.
